# Assumption Analysis Report

## Weakest Assumptions (Most Questionable)

### 🔴 Recursive self-improvement is possible and fast
**Link:** AGI systems recursively self-improve
**Epistemic Score:** -0.30
**Rationale:** Highly speculative. No empirical evidence. Depends on 'low-hanging fruit' in AI research.

**Citations:**
- AI2027: "Compress decade of algorithmic progress into ≤1 year"

**Contested by:**
- Most AI researchers
- Diminishing returns arguments

### 🟡 AGI can automate AI research (ML engineering, theory, debugging)
**Link:** Scaffolding + unhobbling + agentic capabilities emerge
**Epistemic Score:** 0.10
**Rationale:** Highly speculative. Depends on breadth of capabilities.

**Citations:**
- AI2027: "Hundreds of millions of AGIs automate AI research"

**Contested by:**
- Creative research requirements
- Reliability at scale

### 🟡 Chatbot → Agent transition happens smoothly via scaffolding
**Link:** Scaffolding + unhobbling + agentic capabilities emerge
**Epistemic Score:** 0.20
**Rationale:** Speculative. Some evidence from AutoGPT, but reliable agency unproven.

**Citations:**
- Situational Awareness: "By 2027, models will be agents like coworkers, not chatbots"

**Contested by:**
- Reliability concerns
- Long-horizon planning difficulties

### 🟡 Scaling laws continue to hold (no diminishing returns)
**Link:** Continued investment in larger training runs
**Epistemic Score:** 0.30
**Rationale:** Empirically held so far, but theoretical basis weak. Chinchilla scaling suggests continued gains.

**Citations:**
- Scaling Laws Paper: "Performance scales as power law of compute, data, parameters"

**Contested by:**
- Data wall concerns
- Sample efficiency limits

**Citations:**

**Contested by:**
- Diminishing returns arguments
